SplitterGrip

SplitterGrip is SplitContainer's source-private retained seam control. It owns visual/focus/cursor/collapse-tab state while SplitContainer remains the allocation and input-policy authority. Its 7 × 28 rest actuator expands to 12 × 42 on bounded pointer proximity, active press, or keyboard-visible focus.
