Grouping
========

Panels, group boxes, cards, tabs and expandable sections.

.. list-table::
   :class: autosummary longtable
   :widths: 35 65

   * - :cpp:class:`Panel <gui_forms::Panel>`
     - A plain surface that holds and arranges child controls.
   * - :cpp:class:`GroupBox <gui_forms::GroupBox>`
     - A framed group of controls with a caption.
   * - :cpp:class:`ScaledGroupBox <gui_forms::ScaledGroupBox>`
     - A group box whose children keep their proportions as it is resized.
   * - :cpp:class:`MaterialPanel <gui_forms::MaterialPanel>`
     - A panel that paints a surface material of your choosing behind its children.
   * - :cpp:class:`Card <gui_forms::Card>`
     - A themed surface with a header, a body and a footer.
   * - :cpp:class:`ExpandableSections <gui_forms::ExpandableSections>`
     - A vertical stack of sections that the user expands and collapses.
   * - :cpp:class:`ContainerControl <gui_forms::ContainerControl>`
     - The base class for containers that manage focus and validation for their descendants.
   * - :cpp:class:`UserControl <gui_forms::UserControl>`
     - A container for building your own reusable composite control.
   * - :cpp:class:`TabControl <gui_forms::TabControl>`
     - A set of pages shown one at a time, chosen from a row of tabs.
   * - :cpp:class:`PageStrip <gui_forms::PageStrip>`
     - The tab headers of a page set in one row, at the top or the bottom of their content.
   * - :cpp:class:`PageView <gui_forms::PageView>`
     - A row of tabs over the selected page's content: the strip preset of the page view family.
   * - :cpp:class:`NavigationPane <gui_forms::NavigationPane>`
     - An Outlook-style navigation pane: the selected page's content above a stack of large page buttons.
   * - :cpp:class:`TabPage <gui_forms::TabPage>`
     - One page of a tab control, with the caption shown on its tab.

.. cpp:class:: PageControlContent final : public PageContent

   ``#include <gui_forms/controls/pages/page_view/page_view.hpp>``

   The content of one page of a PageView: the application's control, shown in the view's content area while its page is selected. The set owns this record and the record holds the control; the view makes the control a child of the page's panel while the page is in the view's set, so ``take`` and ``put`` move a page's control between views with its state, and removing the page releases it.

.. cpp:struct:: PageInvocation final

   ``#include <gui_forms/controls/pages/page_strip/page_strip.hpp>``

   A tab was clicked, chosen with the keyboard, by its mnemonic, from the page list or through accessibility, and its page is now selected (it may have been selected already). Raised after the selection and its ``selected_page_changed``; never for a programmatic selection and never when a ``selecting`` handler cancelled.
