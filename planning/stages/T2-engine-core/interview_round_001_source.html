What T2 has to prove

The gate, from the stage brief: a reference-image test per primitive on all three hosts with identical pixels; the window counters report zero frames once inputs stop and every settling primitive has settled; a part whose cost cannot be bounded falls back to the built-in recipe; reduced motion disables motion primitives. The accepted starting tokens stay as they are (light angle 135°, overshoot 8 %, blur cap 8 px, ambient 30 fps over at most 10 % of the window, 400 ms ceiling) until a native specimen sheet revisits them.

Already shown by the paused first phase (#161): the bulge and dithered bulge produced the same 36 reference-image hashes and the same 1,536-value math table on macOS arm64, Linux arm64, Linux x64 and Windows x64, using first-party power, exponential and logarithm and no fused multiply-add.
