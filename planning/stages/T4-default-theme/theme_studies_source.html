Default theme studies

The default as decided: Pearl Ribbon moved toward Plan Paint's royale blue, in its light form and its derived dark form, followed by the comparison for the modern flat Windows-familiar theme. Hover and press the controls; press Tab to see keyboard focus.

Decided, and drawn here

  • Identity as in Plan Paint: the blue File tab, the tab rail and the command plane. No fresco band.
  • Ribbon buttons are a dithered bulge: the cloth bulge (a fingertip pushed into the ribbon from behind, lit on the upper-left slope, shaded on the far slope, holding and then falling off at the rim) subsampled onto a lattice of 1 px dots every 2 px. Each slope has a three-step tonal ramp (deep, mid and light blue on the shaded side; pale tint, near-white and white on the lit side) and an ordered 4×4 threshold dithers between neighbouring steps, so the gradation survives; between the dots the ribbon shows through. There is no edge of any kind. Pressed, the dots form a dent. The active tool is the same lattice in gold. Push buttons keep Plan Paint's plate. Recess is deepest at the entry fields and the work pane.
  • Tabs copy Plan Paint's tab stack on a flat #93ADD0 rail: tabs abut, and each casts a hard two-pixel #496C9F edge and a soft shadow onto the next, with a white top line and a double right keyline. The selected tab is near-white, slightly larger, and runs into the ribbon with no bottom edge.
  • The ribbon plane deepens from near-white to royale blue toward its foot, with group labels under fading dividers.
  • Side planes and dialog stock in near-white #F4F4F5 on blue chrome; selection is a semantic blue; keyboard focus is a dotted ring; text fields show focus whenever focused.
  • The only motion on everyday surfaces is the progress bar's glint and its small activity object.
  • The dark variant reaches the whole window, built from Plan Paint's Phosphor pair (black with green #82B361) and its complement #B36182 for the active tool and File tab.

Question 6: icons in the modern flat Windows-familiar theme

The modern flat theme ships beside the default for people who want a current-Windows feel. Your verdicts reject monochrome glyphs in place of material icons (C152). These two windows are the same flat theme; only the icons differ.
